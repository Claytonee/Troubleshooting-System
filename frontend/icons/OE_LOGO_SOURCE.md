# Official Opportunity Education email logo

`oe-logo-official.svg` is the unmodified, light-background horizontal Opportunity Education
wordmark published by Opportunity Education:

https://opportunityeducation.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/OE_Logo2019_H_forLite_RGB.svg

It was retrieved on 25 September 2026. `oe-logo-official.png` is a 4× raster rendering of that
same 300 × 31 SVG for broad email-client compatibility. Account-recovery emails attach the PNG
inline with a content ID; they do not hot-link the source or use a recreated mark.
