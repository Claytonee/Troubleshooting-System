# Opportunity Education logo

`oe-logo-official.svg` is an unmodified copy of `frontend/icons/oe-logo-official.svg`: the official
light-background horizontal logo published by Opportunity Education
(https://opportunityeducation.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/OE_Logo2019_H_forLite_RGB.svg,
retrieved 2026-09-25; see `frontend/icons/OE_LOGO_SOURCE.md`).

The video is dark, so `scripts/build-frames.mjs` draws it with the dark-background treatment defined
in `docs/OE_BRANDING_GUIDE.md`: the sunburst stays gold `#ffae00`, the lettering (`#263746`) becomes
white. The paths themselves are never redrawn or edited.
